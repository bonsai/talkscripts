# Jade can Envision: Crystallized Will and the Horizon of Dynamic Order

In Jade, a program is not a predetermined set of deterministic instructions, but a "dynamic order" that coalesces into the optimal form at the moment of execution.

## Core Concepts
1. **From Plasma to Crystal**: The phase transition of ambiguous intent (Plasma) into structured execution units (Crystals) via AI catalysis.
2. **Meta-Orchestration**: The absence of a fixed conductor. Based on metadata (Shell), it summons the optimal experts and synthesizes a temporary execution plan (Formula) on the fly.
3. **Accumulation of Deltas**: Rejecting the notion of "completion," it continuously optimizes by accumulating minute differences (Deltas) with each execution.

Jade transforms the computer from a "tool" into a "partner" by adding flexible AI muscles and an autonomous nervous system of meta-orchestration to the cold skeleton of logic.
